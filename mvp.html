Stage 1
What's Happening
Aminata found CleanSalone via a neighbour's WhatsApp referral. She's messaging for the first time.

The bot responds instantly — no human needed. This is the zero-friction entry point.

Design decision: WhatsApp was chosen because 62% of survey respondents already use it daily. No new app = no adoption barrier.

Stage 2
Service Type
Two clear options. No jargon. Simple language. The bot uses Krio-friendly English that feels natural in Freetown.

Key insight: Market traders need different pickup frequency than households. Segmentation starts here, not later.

Stage 3
Location Capture
Aminata types her neighbourhood name. No GPS needed. The bot recognises Goderich as a Phase 1 area — covered from Month 1.

Survey data: Goderich and Lumley have the highest willingness-to-pay density — our beachhead market.

Stage 4
Plan & Pricing
Three tiers. Transparent pricing. The Le 30,000/month entry tier is priced below stated WTP of Le 50,000 — to maximise first-trial conversion.

Friction point: This is where users drop off most. Solution: show value first, price second. First 2 weeks FREE.

Stage 5
Payment Flow
Orange Money / Afrimoney. No cash. No bank account needed. The bot sends a pay prompt directly inside WhatsApp.

Trust signal: Payment confirmation SMS arrives instantly. This proof-of-transaction is critical for first-time users.

Stage 6
Booking Confirmed
Aminata gets her booking reference, first pickup date, and collector's name. Named collectors build neighbourhood trust.

Retention driver: Day-before SMS reminder reduces no-shows and builds the habit of putting waste out on collection day.

Stage 7
Green Points Onboarding
The recycling reward is introduced after the core transaction — not during. This keeps the booking flow clean and introduces upsell naturally.

Survey data: 69% of respondents said they'd exchange recyclables for discounts or cash. This is a retention multiplier.
