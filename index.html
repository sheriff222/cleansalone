Our Mission
Reliable, affordable waste collection for every Freetown household — with recycling rewards that turn waste into income.
01
No Reliable System
Government trucks are absent. Private collectors are inconsistent. People burn or dump waste — the most dangerous options available.
02
Affordability Trap
Most families cannot afford consistent collection. Waste services compete with food. People know burning is harmful — they have no affordable alternative.
03
Dignity Lost
Residents don't want to live surrounded by waste. They simply have no other choice. We give them that choice — on their terms.

What We
Actually Build

A two-phase MVP designed around real community needs — not assumptions.
📲
WhatsApp & USSD Booking
Schedule pickups via WhatsApp or basic USSD — no smartphone, no app download required. Works for everyone.
📅
Scheduled Weekly Pickup
Reliable, consistent collection routes. SMS reminders the day before. No guessing when the truck is coming.
♻️
Green Points Recycling
Sort your recyclables and earn Green Points — convert them to mobile money or subscription discounts. Waste becomes income.
💵
Mobile Money Payments
Pay via Orange Money or Afrimoney. Affordable tiered pricing starting from Le 30,000/month for households.
